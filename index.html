Postdoctoral Fellow DV3M Project University of Exeter Volcano Deformation Numerical Modelling Hazard Forecasting

Research Overview

I apply physics-based numerical models to interpret the dynamics of active volcanoes and reduce uncertainty in eruption forecasting.

Research Focus

Transcrustal magmatic systems, dynamic magma-mush rheology, and the interface between poroelastic reservoirs and deforming crust.

Methodology

Integrate InSAR, GNSS, petrology, gas, and field observations with thermo-poroelastic simulations to model unrest signals.

Field Campaigns

Montserrat and Sakurajima.
